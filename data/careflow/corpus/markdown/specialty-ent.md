### 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This document establishes mandatory operational protocols for the Otolaryngology (ENT) service line across all Meridian Health Partners facilities. Compliance is mandatory for all scheduling staff, clinical intake coordinators, and attending otolaryngologists.

### 2. CONDITIONS ACCEPTED AND EXPLICITLY NOT ACCEPTED

**2.1 Accepted Conditions**
The ENT department accepts adult and pediatric patients (aged 24 months and older) presenting with the following diagnosed or suspected conditions:
*   Chronic rhinosinusitis refractory to primary care management.
*   Persistent conductive or sensorineural hearing loss.
*   Recurrent acute tonsillitis (meeting Paradise criteria).
*   Benign paroxysmal positional vertigo (BPPV) and peripheral vestibular disorders.
*   Dysphonia and suspected vocal cord lesions.
*   Obstructive sleep apnea (OSA) requiring diagnostic evaluation or non-surgical management.

**2.2 Explicitly Not Accepted Conditions**
The following conditions are outside the scope of Meridian’s outpatient ENT service line and must be redirected via direct transfer protocols to tertiary acute care facilities:
*   Acute penetrating or blunt trauma to the head and neck.
*   Active, profuse epistaxis requiring surgical arterial ligation or posterior nasal packing.
*   Suspected or confirmed advanced-stage head and neck malignancies requiring immediate radical resection or concurrent chemoradiotherapy.
*   Deep neck space infections (e.g., Ludwig’s angina, retropharyngeal abscess).
*   Foreign body aspirations involving the lower respiratory tract.

### 3. APPOINTMENT DURATIONS
All scheduling slots must adhere strictly to the following temporal allocations:
*   **New Patient Consultations:** 40 minutes.
*   **Established Patient Return Visits:** 20 minutes.
*   **Procedure-Specific Slots (Flexible Laryngoscopy, Nasal Endoscopy, Audiometry):** 30 minutes.
*   **Post-Operative Global Period Check (Days 7–14):** 15 minutes.

### 4. PATIENT PREPARATION INSTRUCTIONS
Scheduling staff must issue the following preparation directives at the time of booking:
*   **Vestibular Testing (VNG/ENG):** Patients must cease consumption of alcoholic beverages, sedatives, and antihistamines **48 hours** prior to the appointment. Fasting is required for **4 hours** immediately preceding the test.
*   **Audiometry:** Patients must avoid exposure to high-intensity occupational or recreational noise (e.g., power tools, heavy machinery, personal listening devices above 80 decibels) for **14 hours** prior to audiometric evaluation.
*   **In-Office Endoscopy:** No dietary restrictions apply, but patients must be informed that mild transient gagging or epistaxis may occur.

### 6. REFERRAL TURNAROUND COMMITMENT
*   **6.1 Standard Referrals:** The department commits to contacting the patient to schedule an appointment within **72 hours** of receiving a complete referral packet.
*   **6.2 Urgent Referrals (e.g., sudden sensorineural hearing loss, rapid onset dysphagia without trauma):** The department commits to direct telephonic contact and appointment booking within **8 hours** of receipt.
*   **6.3 Incomplete Referrals:** If clinical documentation is missing, the referral coordinator must notify the originating clinic within **24 hours**; processing pauses until receipt of addendum.
