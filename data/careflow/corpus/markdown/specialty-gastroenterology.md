### 4.1 Purpose and Scope
This document establishes operational standards, clinical inclusion criteria, scheduling parameters, and insurance coverage matrices for the Gastroenterology Service Line at Meridian Health Partners. Compliance with these operational clauses is mandatory for all clinic personnel.

---

### 4.2 Conditions Accepted and Explicitly Not Accepted

**4.2.1 Accepted Conditions**  
The Gastroenterology service line accepts ambulatory adult patients (aged 18 and older) presenting with the following confirmed or suspected primary conditions:  
1. Chronic gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD) refractory to primary care management.  
2. Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD), specifically Crohn’s disease and ulcerative colitis, in stable or moderately active phases.  
3. Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS) requiring specialized dietary or pharmacologic intervention.  
4. Screening, surveillance, and diagnostic evaluations for colorectal polyps.  
5. Chronic viral hepatitis (Types B and C) without acute hepatic decompensation.  

**4.2.2 Explicitly Not Accepted Conditions**  
To maintain outpatient facility licensure and safety thresholds, the following conditions are excluded from all 14 Meridian Health Partners outpatient sites and must be redirected immediately to regional acute-care tertiary facilities:  
1. Acute gastrointestinal hemorrhage requiring hemodynamic resuscitation or active blood transfusion.  
2. Fulminant hepatic failure or acute-on-chronic liver failure exhibiting encephalopathy grade III or IV.  
3. Acute abdominal emergencies, including suspected bowel perforation, acute pancreatitis, or toxic megacolon.  
4. Pediatric gastroenterology cases involving patients under the age of 18 (refer to Meridian Pediatric Alliance).  
5. Bariatric surgery evaluations and post-surgical bariatric revisions (refer to Meridian Metabolic Institute).

### 4.3 Appointment Durations

Scheduling templates for the Gastroenterology department must strictly adhere to the following slot allocations:  
1. **Initial New Patient Consultations:** Allocated exactly **45 minutes** per appointment.  
2. **Established Patient Follow-Up Visits:** Allocated exactly **20 minutes** per appointment.  
3. **Procedure Pre-Evaluation / Anesthesia Screening:** Allocated exactly **30 minutes** per appointment.  

---

### 4.4 Patient Preparation Instructions

All clinical staff must issue the following standardized pre-procedure instructions based on the scheduled modality:  
1. **Upper Endoscopy (EGD):** Patients must observe a strict nil per os (NPO) status, consuming no solid foods or clear liquids for a minimum of **8 hours** prior to the scheduled procedure arrival time.  
2. **Lower Endoscopy (Colonoscopy):** Patients must ingest the full prescribed polyethylene glycol electrolyte lavage solution split-dose regimen, consuming the first dose the evening before the procedure (starting precisely at 18:00) and the second dose **4 hours** prior to the morning arrival time.  
3. **Medication Adjustments:** Patients prescribed therapeutic anticoagulants or antiplatelet agents must receive clearance from prescribing cardiology or neurology specialists to withhold these agents **5 business days** prior to any scheduled endoscopic procedure.

### 4.6 Referral Turnaround Commitment

**4.6.1 Processing Timelines**  
The Gastroenterology department commits to a strict internal service level agreement (SLA) for all incoming provider-to-provider referrals:  
1. **Routine Referrals:** All routine referrals received via the electronic health record must be triaged, reviewed by a licensed mid-level provider or gastroenterologist, and scheduled or declined within **3 business days** of receipt.  
2. **Urgent Referrals:** Referrals designated as urgent by the primary provider due to severe symptomology or rapid weight loss must be triaged and contacted for scheduling within **24 hours** of receipt.  

**4.6.2 Named Exception**  
Referrals originating from outside the Meridian Health Partners integrated network that require external primary insurance authorization verification are exempt from the standard 3-business-day timeline. Such deferred cases shall be processed within a secondary window of **7 business days** to allow for external payer administrative clearance.
