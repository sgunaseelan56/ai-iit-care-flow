**Effective Date:** January 15, 2024  
**Applies To:** All Pulmonology Department Staff, Central Scheduling, and Referral Coordinators across all 14 Meridian Health Partners sites.  
**Policy Lead:** Dr. Aris Thorne, Operations Policy Lead, Meridian Health Partners  

---

### SECTION 1: DEPARTMENT SCOPE AND CONDITIONS ACCEPTED

**1.1** The Pulmonology Department at Meridian Health Partners provides outpatient consultation, diagnostic testing, and chronic management for respiratory and sleep-related conditions across all 14 clinic locations.

**1.2 Accepted Conditions:** The department accepts referrals and scheduled visits for the following confirmed or suspected primary diagnoses:
*   Asthma (ICD-10 J45 series)
*   Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease, including chronic bronchitis and emphysema (ICD-10 J43-J44 series)
*   Obstructive and Central Sleep Apnea (ICD-10 G47.3 series)
*   Bronchiectasis (ICD-10 J47)
*   Interstitial Lung Disease and Pulmonary Fibrosis (ICD-10 J84 series)
*   Pulmonary Hypertension, WHO Groups 1 through 5 (ICD-10 I27.0, I27.2 series)
*   Chronic Cough exceeding eight weeks duration of undetermined etiology

**1.3 Explicitly Not Accepted Conditions:** The Pulmonology Department does not accept direct outpatient referrals for, and will immediately redirect or refuse, the following patient profiles:
*   Acute respiratory failure requiring mechanical ventilation or immediate inpatient admission.
*   Active, untreated pulmonary tuberculosis (ICD-10 A15-A16 series); such patients must be referred directly to the county public health tuberculosis clinic.
*   Pediatric patients under the age of 12 years; pediatric pulmonary care is outside the clinical scope of adult multi-specialty outpatient operations at Meridian Health Partners.
*   Massive hemoptysis defined as expectoration of greater than **200 mL** of blood within a 24-hour period; these cases require emergency department triage.

### SECTION 2: APPOINTMENT DURATION AND SCHEDULING TIMELINES

**2.1** Standardized appointment durations are mandatory across all 14 Meridian Health Partners sites to maintain master schedule integrity. Schedulers shall allocate time as follows:
*   **New Patient Consultation:** Exactly **45 minutes** per encounter.
*   **Established Patient Follow-Up:** Exactly **20 minutes** per encounter.
*   **Diagnostic Pulmonary Function Testing (PFT) Only:** Exactly **30 minutes** per encounter.

**2.2 Named Exceptions:** Patients scheduled under the Interstitial Lung Disease (ILD) pathway managed by designated pulmonologist Dr. Helen Vance receive an allocated initial consultation window of **60 minutes** to account for multi-system chart review and caregiver integration.

---

### SECTION 3: PATIENT PREPARATION INSTRUCTIONS

**3.1** Central Scheduling and the referring intake team must issue the following standardized preparation instructions to patients prior to diagnostic pulmonary function testing:
*   **Bronchodilator Withholding:** Short-acting beta-agonists (e.g., Albuterol) must be withheld for a minimum of **6 hours** prior to testing. Long-acting bronchodilators (e.g., Tiotropium, Salmeterol) must be withheld for **24 hours** prior to testing.
*   **Substance Restrictions:** Patients must refrain from smoking tobacco or consuming caffeinated beverages (coffee, tea, cola, energy drinks) for at least **4 hours** prior to any scheduled PFT or arterial blood gas appointment.
*   **Dietary Restrictions:** Patients undergoing overnight diagnostic sleep studies (polysomnography) conducted at affiliated Meridian sleep suites must avoid heavy meals, alcohol, and sedating medications not explicitly cleared by the attending physician for **6 hours** prior to study check-in.

### SECTION 5: REFERRAL TURNAROUND COMMITMENT

**5.1** The Pulmonology Department maintains a strict internal operational service level agreement (SLA) for inbound clinical referrals. 

**5.2** Routine referrals transmitted via the Meridian electronic health record portal must be triaged, reviewed by a mid-level provider or attending physician, and scheduled or formally declined within **48 business hours** of receipt.

**5.3** Urgent referrals bearing explicit clinical flags for severe dyspnea or suspected malignancy must be triaged and contacted for scheduling within **12 business hours** of receipt. Failure to meet these turnaround thresholds triggers an automated administrative review by operations manager Sarah Jenkins.
